**pikku-build** gains `references/design.md`: the composition doctrine that no theme can supply. An app built to the letter of the skill still came out looking like nobody had decided anything — a form at the top of every list screen, sixty identical cards each carrying two filled buttons, an "urgent" group holding half the fridge, a scaffolded login page with a Google button the app had no credentials for, and the whole thing judged against a dev database full of `Ripe peaches d193e2aa` left behind by scenario runs. The new reference names the bar (six questions a screen has to answer), the composition rules that get it there, the screens agents forget — login, navigation, empty states — and the reset-and-restart that has to happen before any of it is judged. §8 now says out loud that half of it belongs at §6, because a design pass over eight finished milestones is a repaint.
