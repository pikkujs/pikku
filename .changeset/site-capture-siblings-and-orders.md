The site capture records the site's own other hosts it links to (an `app.` or `info.` sub-domain) under `siblings`, each with its first page opened once, read-only and without signing in; it never lists or opens a shop's cart and checkout links (`/buy/…`, `/checkout…`, `/cart/add…`); a template is a group of twenty or more alike pages and never a language version or a CMS's own pages; German flow words (Buchen, Anmelden, Bewerben…) start a flow; and a hosted form that reads its fields with a POST is recorded with them.
