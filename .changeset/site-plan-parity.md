Planning from a site's notes: the rebuilt site does what the original does, so what only a policy text or a service's name suggests is an open question, not a change, route, table or email; every outside service and every outside host the capture saw is named by a change with a replace or keep decision; the domain move is an open question in the data change; and no capture change is filed when the capture is already there.

Every change of a site's plan ends with an `Evidence:` list naming, per feature, the captured page, `behaviour.json` key, site note or captured URL it rests on; a feature with no source is an open question.
