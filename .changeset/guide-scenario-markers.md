A guide marker can name one scenario — `<!-- pikku:guide feature=F scenario=S -->` — so each section of a page shows its own figures instead of every scenario's landing in one block. Showcase shots now lead a scenario's figures, and a recording is captioned with its actor only when there are several.

The pikku-guide skill now plans pages from the suite, puts one `scenario=` marker under each section, gives every cited scenario a closing still, and treats captions and seed data on camera as copy.
